2What is in a chord?

Key idea

A chord is three or more notes played at the same time. A triad is a three-note chord built from a root, a third and a fifth.
A semitone is the distance from one key to the very next key (black or white).
  • Major triad = root, up 4 semitones, up 3 more. Sounds bright or happy.
  • Minor triad = root, up 3 semitones, up 4 more. Sounds dark or sad.
